Our Products

Engineered Stone & Aggregate Products for Stronger Infrastructure

  • 01
    M-Sand (Manufactured Sand)
    Crushed sand manufactured to construction standards for concrete and structural works.
    Processed Sand

    Advantages

    • Consistent grading ensures uniform concrete strength and durability
    • Zero organic impurities improve long-term structural stability
    • Higher compressive strength compared to natural sand
    • Eco-friendly alternative that reduces dependency on river sand

    Best Used For

    • Concrete mix design (M20–M50) for reliable performance
    • RCC structural works including beams and columns
    • Foundation and slab construction requiring strength
    • High-rise buildings and large-scale infrastructure projects
  • 02
    P-Sand (Plastering Sand)
    Finely graded sand processed for plastering, tiling, and smooth surface applications.
    Processed Sand

    Advantages

    • Uniform fine texture ensures smooth and even plaster finish
    • Excellent workability makes application faster and easier
    • Reduces wall cracking and shrinkage issues
    • Good water retention improves bonding with cement

    Best Used For

    • Internal and external wall plastering applications
    • Tile adhesive mortar for better grip and finish
    • Block jointing with improved bonding strength
    • Smooth surface finishing for decorative works
  • 03
    20 MM Crushed Stone Aggregates
    Processed stone aggregates for concrete mixing and standard RCC works.
    Aggregate

    Advantages

    • Optimum size ensures strong and stable RCC structures
    • High crushing resistance improves durability under load
    • Angular shape provides better bonding with cement paste
    • Low flakiness index ensures consistent performance

    Best Used For

    • Concrete production (M15–M35) for general construction
    • Beams, columns, and slabs in RCC structures
    • Precast elements like blocks and panels
    • Drainage layers with good permeability
  • 04
    40 MM Crushed Stone Aggregates
    Coarse aggregates for road foundations and heavy infrastructure applications.
    Aggregate

    Advantages

    • High load-bearing capacity suitable for heavy-duty applications
    • Excellent drainage properties prevent water accumulation
    • Durable under heavy traffic and harsh conditions
    • Cost-effective solution for large-scale construction

    Best Used For

    • Road sub-base and base layer construction
    • Mass concrete works requiring bulk aggregates
    • Retaining walls and large structural foundations
    • Heavy infrastructure and industrial projects
  • 05
    12 MM Crushed Stone Aggregates
    Fine stone aggregates for concrete production and compact surface finishes.
    Aggregate

    Advantages

    • Ideal for thin concrete sections with precise application
    • Provides smooth and high-quality surface finish
    • Easy to compact and place in tight areas
    • Highly versatile for different mix designs

    Best Used For

    • Flooring and surface concrete applications
    • Paver blocks and decorative concrete works
    • Thin RCC sections with detailed finishing
    • Bituminous mix designs in road construction
  • 06
    Wet Mix Macadam (WMM)
    Mechanically mixed aggregates for road base and sub-base layers.
    Road Works

    Advantages

    • Superior interlocking structure for strong base formation
    • Excellent bearing capacity for heavy loads
    • Meets MoRTH specifications for quality assurance
    • Provides a long-lasting and stable base layer

    Best Used For

    • National and state highway construction projects
    • Airport runways requiring strong base layers
    • Industrial road pavements with heavy traffic
    • Urban road and infrastructure development
